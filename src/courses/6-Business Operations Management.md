Description: >
  Operations management is at the heart of every organisation. It's the activity
  of managing the resources devoted to creating and delivering services and
  products - the core functions of any business. This course provides a broad
  introduction to operations management and the associated tools and techniques
  that support the design, planning, control and improvement of systems and
  processes. The course will help you develop an awareness of the different
  aspects of operations, including; process flow, performance objectives,
  scheduling, supply chains and inventory, layout, uncertainty and risk. You
  will explore the ways in which these can be analysed and represented to
  improve business decision-making. You will develop the systematic and critical
  thinking skills essential to understanding, managing and improving operations
  in a broad range of industries and business contexts that you may find
  yourself in.

Assessments:
  - Name: Weekly practicals
    Weighting: 24
    Type:
      - Problem Solving
    Description: >-
      This assessment involves a weekly submission where learners demonstrate
      their ability to apply the various decision tools and models demonstrated
      in the weekly module. 
  - Name: Reflections
    Weighting: 35
    Type:
      - Learning Journal
    Description: >-
      The reflections task is an opportunity for learners to reflect on the
      feedback from their practical tasks and the material presented in the
      course. Learners can use this space to discuss their understanding (or
      lack of) the course content and concepts, helping to inform the teacher of
      their needs and requirements.
  - Name: Critical analysis
    Weighting: 16
    Type:
      - Critical Analysis
    Description: >-
      Learners are required to select and review an academic paper and provide a
      succinct and constructively critical review of its content. A particular
      focus is paid to identifying and reviewing the use of models used in the
      study.
  - Name: Case study
    Weighting: 25
    Type:
      - Case Study
    Description: >-
      Leaners are asked research an existing business venture or operation. They
      are asked to to enrich their analysis by employing several techniques to
      structure the problem and examine the system being considered. They are
      required to look at the broader system and the techniques and models
      employed. As a result of their investigation, they are required to make
      recommendations regarding the design, implementation or improvement of the
      operation.
